# Possible Zones (brainstorm, not built)

Just pitches — no code, no data entries. Format mirrors `data/zones.ts`'s
`monsters: [{ id, weight }]` shape for at-a-glance vibes, not final numbers.

## rainbowBog

*"The water reflects more colors than are physically present here. Local
guides insist this is fine."*

```
rainbowBog: [
  watersnake: 10,
  fox: 8,
  moose: 12,
  blueberry: 3,
  duckJustADuck: 6,
  deceptiveMoundLookingSolidButWasActuallyWetFeet: 1,
]
```

- **Blueberry** — a single, furious blueberry. Nobody knows why. It won.
- **Duck. Just a Duck.** — every other entry in this zone is deranged in some
  specific way. This is a duck. That's the joke.
- **Deceptive Mound (Looking Solid But Was Actually Wet Feet)** — not a
  creature, an indignity. You stepped on it with confidence. The fight is
  against your own socks now.

## theLastLedger

A collapsed trading post, reclaimed informally. *"Commerce continues,
without the building."* Natural home for **Ruffian** (shelved from Whispering
Woods — a bandit squatting in a ruin makes sense; a bandit standing around in
pure animal forest didn't).

```
theLastLedger: [
  feralGoat: 10,
  ruffian: 8,
  suspiciouslyOrganizedRatKing: 3,
  guyWhoDefinitelyOwnsThisNow: 4,
  theAuditor: 1,
]
```

- **Feral Goat** — goats colonize every abandoned human structure on Earth,
  no exceptions. Universal law, not a joke, just true.
- **Suspiciously Organized Rat King** — the rats have formed something
  resembling a functioning economy. Concerning, mostly for the rats' sake.
- **Guy Who Definitely Owns This Now** — no paperwork. No deed. Full
  confidence.
- **The Auditor** — a ghost that insists you settle a debt that no longer
  legally exists, on principle. Continues the vendor-ledger joke already
  living in `loot.ts`'s `"chicken"` flavor text.
